The scalable idea

Research once.
Personalize safely.

The platform does not repeat expensive market research for every user. Shared market intelligence is created once, then a smaller deterministic evaluation maps each material change to the portfolios it actually affects.

01Observe

Prices, filings, events, and licensed news enter one shared pipeline.

→
02Verify

Normalize facts, calculate features, and retain timestamps and evidence.

→
03Match

Evaluate only affected holdings against each user's limits and policy.

→
04Explain

Deliver a concise brief with risks, reasons, and data-quality status.

i

Phase 1 is deterministic by design. The current Telegram MVP proves the interaction and analytics. Shared live research and grounded AI explanations are later gated milestones—not claims about the code today.

The trust layer

Useful without pretending to predict.

The startup opportunity is not another black-box stock picker. It is an independent, cross-broker decision record users can inspect and challenge.

01

Math before language

Portfolio values, weights, gains, and risk metrics stay deterministic and testable.

02

Evidence with timestamps

Future decision cards must show where each material fact came from and when it was observed.

03

Risk before action

Concentration, stale data, and policy conflicts are surfaced before opportunity language.

04

Execution stays gated

Live trading remains outside the product until technical, legal, and regulatory gates are passed.
